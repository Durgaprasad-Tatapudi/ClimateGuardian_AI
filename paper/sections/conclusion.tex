\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
This study introduces ClimateGuardian AI, a sophisticated hybrid framework establishing a deployable mechanism for multi-hazard climate risk prediction. Through rigorous chronological partitioning and the strict exclusion of target-leaking proxy variables, the evaluation isolates true predictive generalization. The Random Forest and LightGBM methodologies generated robust independent baselines for Flood and Heatwave risks, respectively. While the offline GRU sequence model provided theoretically superior compound-hazard discrimination, the integration of a Logistic Regression Stacking Ensemble provided a vital, stateless alternative bridging the deployment gap for realtime Open-Meteo inference. Ultimately, by systematically demonstrating the end-to-end transition from leakage-aware historical preprocessing to live architectural execution, ClimateGuardian AI represents a practical, highly resilient framework for operational disaster-risk mitigation.
