# Claims Safety Audit

## A. Directly Supported
- Operational/Realtime capable
- Leakage-free evaluation
- Multi-hazard prediction
- Temporal variables drive predictability
- Calibration improves reliability

## B. Supported with Qualification
- Statistically robust (instead of highly accurate)
- The Stacking Ensemble provides a balance between predictive discrimination and stateless realtime deployment feasibility.
- SHAP values characterize the model's internal feature-dependency hierarchy.

## C. MUST NOT BE CLAIMED
- 100% accuracy
- Perfect prediction
- Best model in every category
- Algorithmic novelty of baseline ML models
- SHAP physical causality
- Realtime output = historical accuracy
- GRU as operational model
